· Bookshelf

Books that shaped my thinking.

A small collection of reads on mindset, growth, and curiosity that I keep coming back to.

01 Currently Reading

Why Bharat Matters

S. Jaishankar

A compelling look at India's place in the modern world order through diplomacy, history, and civilizational identity. It's reshaping how I think about geopolitics and national purpose.

02 · Mindset

The Power of Your Subconscious Mind

Joseph Murphy

A timeless guide to harnessing the power of belief and mental programming. It shifted my perspective on how deeply our inner thoughts shape our outer reality and daily decisions.

03 · Curiosity

A Brief History of Time

Stephen Hawking

A fascinating exploration of the universe — from black holes to the Big Bang — written with extraordinary clarity. It reignited my sense of wonder and reminded me why curiosity is the most important trait.

04 · Growth

Atomic Habits

James Clear

An insightful breakdown of how tiny, daily routines compound into massive results over time. It's helped me structure both my work habits and personal routines for consistency and long-term growth.
